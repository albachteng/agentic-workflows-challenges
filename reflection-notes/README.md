# Reflection Notes

This folder is for your overall lab reflections and comparative analysis across all exercises.

## What to Document

After completing all six exercises, use this space to:

1. **Compare approaches** - Which exercise methods worked best?
2. **Identify patterns** - What consistently helped or hurt?
3. **Synthesize learnings** - Big picture insights
4. **Make recommendations** - What would you do in production?
5. **Document evolution** - How did your thinking change?

## Files to Create

### Required

- `general-template.md` - Provided template for overall reflection
- `workflow-recommendations.md` - Your recommended practices
- `agent-capabilities-assessment.md` - What agents can/can't do

### Optional

- `transcripts/` - Key conversations from all exercises
- `comparative-analysis.md` - Cross-exercise comparison
- `lessons-learned.md` - Actionable takeaways
- `future-experiments.md` - What you'd test next

## Using the Template

1. Complete `general-template.md` after finishing all exercises
2. Be honest about what worked and what didn't
3. Provide specific examples from your experience
4. Make actionable recommendations
5. Share with your cohort for discussion

## Sharing

These reflections are meant to be shared and discussed:

- What patterns did you discover?
- What surprised you?
- What would you do differently?
- What questions remain unanswered?

Your honest assessment helps everyone learn!
